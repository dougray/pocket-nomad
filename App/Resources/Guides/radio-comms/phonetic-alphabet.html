Do this now

  1. Spell any important word letter by letter using these code words. Example: "Smith: Sierra Mike India Tango Hotel."
  2. Say numbers one digit at a time: "one five zero" for 150.
  3. Use it for names, street names, call signs, and anything that could be misheard.

9Nine ("niner")

"Fife" and "niner" are used in aviation, military, and marine radio to avoid mixing up five/fire and nine/no (German "nein"). Plain "five" and "nine" are fine on most other radios.

Practice by spelling your name, street, and town out loud. In a stressful moment, it will come easily.
