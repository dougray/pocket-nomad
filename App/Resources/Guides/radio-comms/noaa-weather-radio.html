Do this now

  1. Turn on your NOAA Weather Radio, or a radio with a "WX" weather band.
  2. Scan the 7 weather channels (162.400 to 162.550 MHz) and pick the clearest one.
  3. Turn on alert mode so it stays silent until a warning comes.
  4. If your radio supports SAME, program your county code so you only get alerts for your area.
  5. Keep fresh batteries in it, or use a hand-crank or solar model.

What it is

NOAA Weather Radio All Hazards is a network of radio stations run by the National Weather Service. It broadcasts forecasts and warnings 24 hours a day. It also carries alerts for other hazards, like chemical spills and AMBER alerts. No license needed. It is receive only.

Radio makers don't all number the channels the same way. Go by the frequency if your radio shows it.

SAME codes

SAME (Specific Area Message Encoding) codes are 6-digit numbers for each county. The NWS website lists them. Program the code for your county, plus nearby counties if storms usually come from that direction.

Many ham, GMRS, and scanner radios can also receive weather channels. Save them in memory.
