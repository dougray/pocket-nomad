Do this now

  1. Check that the scene is safe. Call 911 and report how many people are hurt.
  2. Shout: "If you can walk, come to me!" Those who walk over are GREEN (minor). Ask them to help.
  3. Go to each remaining person. Spend less than about 30 seconds each.
  4. Breathing? If not, open the airway once. Still not breathing: BLACK. Starts breathing: RED.
  5. Breathing faster than 30 breaths a minute: RED.
  6. No pulse at the wrist, or nail bed takes more than 2 seconds to pink up after pressing: RED. Stop major bleeding now.
  7. Can't follow simple commands ("Squeeze my hand"): RED. Can follow them: YELLOW.
  8. Tag or mark each person. Move on. Come back when everyone has been seen.
Call 911 (or your local emergency number) as soon as you can. If you are alone with an adult, call first. If you can, put the phone on speaker so the dispatcher can coach you. This guide does not replace hands-on first aid training. Take a class from the Red Cross or American Heart Association when you can.

START: Simple Triage and Rapid Treatment

When there are more injured people than helpers, triage sorts them so the most people can be saved. It feels hard. It is the right thing to do. Use it until professional responders take over.

The categories

TagMeaningWho
RED ImmediateLife-threatening, needs help firstBreathing over 30/min, no wrist pulse or slow refill, can't follow commands, started breathing after airway opened
YELLOW DelayedSerious but can wait a littleCan't walk but breathing, pulse, and mental status are OK
GREEN Minor"Walking wounded"Can walk
BLACK Expectant / deceasedNot breathing after airway openedLittle can be done with the help available

Memory aid: RPM, 30-2-can do: Respirations over 30, Perfusion over 2 seconds, Mental status "can't do" commands. Any one makes the person RED.

Only quick fixes during triage

  • Open the airway (tilt head back, lift chin).
  • Control severe bleeding with a tourniquet or by having a GREEN helper press on the wound.
  • Put an unconscious breathing person on their side.

Don't stop to do CPR during triage when there are many victims. CPR takes one rescuer away from everyone else.

Marking

  • Use colored tape, ribbon, or a marker on the forehead or arm.
  • Re-check people. Their category can change. Yellow can become red.
Children: a related system called JumpSTART adjusts the breathing numbers for kids. If you are not trained in it, use START and lean toward RED for a child who seems very sick.
