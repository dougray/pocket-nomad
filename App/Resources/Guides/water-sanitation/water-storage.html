Do this now

  1. Store at least 1 gallon (about 4 L) per person per day.
  2. Keep at least a 3-day supply for evacuation and a 2-week supply at home if you can.
  3. Add more for pets, hot weather, nursing mothers, sick people, and children.
  4. Use store-bought bottled water, or clean food-grade containers.
  5. Store it in a cool, dark place. Replace water you bottled yourself every 6 months.

Containers

  • Best: unopened commercial bottled water. Keep it until the date on the label.
  • Food-grade water containers from camping or emergency stores.
  • Clean 2-liter soda bottles with tight caps.
  • Don't use milk or juice jugs (hard to clean, they break down), glass (breaks), or any container that held chemicals.

Cleaning containers before filling

  1. Wash with dish soap and water. Rinse well.
  2. Sanitize: mix 1 teaspoon of unscented bleach in 1 quart (1 L) of water. Pour it in, cap, and shake so it touches every inside surface.
  3. Wait at least 30 seconds, then pour it out. Let it air dry or rinse with clean, safe water.

Filling

  • Fill with safe tap water. If your water comes from a well or is not treated with chlorine, add 2 drops of unscented bleach per gallon.
  • Don't touch the inside of the cap or bottle.
  • Label with the date. Keep away from gasoline, pesticides, and sunlight.

Hidden water in your home

  • Water heater: turn off the power or gas first. Close the cold water inlet. Open a hot water faucet to let air in, then drain from the valve at the bottom. Let it cool first. Sediment at the bottom is normal.
  • Pipes: open the highest faucet in the house to let air in, then drain from the lowest faucet.
  • Ice cubes and the water in canned fruits and vegetables.
  • Toilet tank (not the bowl) only if it has no colored cleaners. Treat it before drinking.
If you hear reports that the water system is broken or polluted, shut off your main water valve to keep dirty water out of your pipes and water heater.
Don't ration water. Drink what you need today and look for more tomorrow. Cut back on activity and stay cool to need less.
