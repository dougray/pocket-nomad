Do this now

  1. If the sewer is working and you have water, you can flush by pouring a bucket of water (about 1 to 2 gallons, 4 to 8 L) quickly into the bowl.
  2. If sewer lines may be broken or backed up (flood, earthquake), don't flush. Use a bag-lined toilet instead.
  3. Line a bucket (or your toilet bowl, after bailing it dry) with two heavy-duty plastic bags.
  4. After each use, cover the waste with an absorbent like kitty litter, sawdust, or shredded paper.
  5. When the bag is about half full, tie it tightly, put it in a covered can, and store it away from people and water.
  6. Wash hands with soap and water every time.

The two-bucket system

Separating urine from solid waste makes less smell and fewer full bags.

  • Bucket 1, urine: a bucket with a lid. Urine from healthy people carries few germs. Where allowed, dilute it and pour it on the ground away from water sources, homes, and gardens with food crops. Or pour it down a working drain.
  • Bucket 2, solids: a bag-lined bucket with a tight lid. Cover each use with sawdust, peat, kitty litter, or dry dirt. Toilet paper goes here too.
  • Label the buckets clearly. Keep lids on.

Bag-lined bucket kit

  • 5-gallon (20 L) bucket with a tight lid, or a toilet seat made to fit one.
  • Heavy-duty trash bags (contractor bags work well).
  • Absorbent: kitty litter, sawdust, or shredded paper.
  • Toilet paper, hand sanitizer, soap, gloves, and a bag of extra bags.
  • A second large covered can for storing full bags.

Disposal

  • Follow official local instructions when they are given.
  • Until then, store sealed bags in a covered container outside, away from water sources and where kids and animals can't reach them.
  • Don't bury bags of waste near water or gardens.
Add a little disinfectant to the bag only if you have it. Never mix bleach with other cleaners or with urine-heavy waste in a closed space, which can release harmful gas. Good airflow helps.
Poor toilet hygiene is a top cause of disease after disasters. Keep the toilet area clean, private, safe at night (add a light), and set up a handwashing station right next to it.
