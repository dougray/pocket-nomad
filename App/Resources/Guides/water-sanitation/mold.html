Do this now

  1. Dry out your home as fast as you can, within 24 to 48 hours. Open doors and windows. Use fans if you have power.
  2. Wear an N95 respirator, goggles, and long rubber gloves while cleaning.
  3. Throw out soaked items that can't be cleaned and dried: carpet, mattresses, upholstered furniture, drywall, insulation.
  4. Scrub mold off hard surfaces with soap and water, or with 1 cup of bleach per 5 gallons (19 L) of water.
  5. Keep children, people with asthma or breathing problems, and people with weak immune systems away during cleanup.

Signs of mold

  • Spots of black, green, white, or other colors on walls, ceilings, or furniture.
  • A musty, earthy smell.
  • Water stains, peeling paint, or warped surfaces.

Health effects

Mold can cause stuffy nose, wheezing, red or itchy eyes, and skin rash. People with asthma, allergies, or weak immune systems can get much sicker.

Cleaning tips

  • Never mix bleach with ammonia or other cleaners.
  • Open windows and doors for fresh air when using bleach.
  • Drywall and insulation that got wet usually need to be cut out at least a little above the water line.
  • Don't just paint over mold. It will come back.
  • If the moldy area is large (more than about 10 square feet, or about 1 square meter), consider professional help.
Never run a gas generator, pressure washer, or other engine inside a home or garage while drying it out. Carbon monoxide can kill.
