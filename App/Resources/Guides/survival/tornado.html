Do this now

  1. Tornado warning or you see one: take shelter now. Don't wait to see it.
  2. Go to a basement or storm shelter. If none, go to the lowest floor, in a small room in the middle of the building with no windows: closet, bathroom, or hallway.
  3. Get under something sturdy. Cover your head and neck with your arms, a mattress, blankets, or a helmet.
  4. Mobile homes are not safe. Leave for a sturdy building or storm shelter before the storm arrives.
  5. Stay in shelter until the warning ends.

Watch vs. warning

AlertMeaningWhat to do
Tornado WatchTornadoes are possible in the areaGet ready. Charge phones. Know where you will go.

Warning signs

  • A dark, often greenish sky. Large hail.
  • A low, dark, spinning cloud, especially if it is lowering.
  • A loud roar like a freight train.
  • Tornadoes can be hidden by rain or come at night. Don't rely on seeing one.

Caught outside or in a car

  • If you can, drive to the nearest sturdy building.
  • Don't hide under a highway overpass. Winds speed up there.
  • If flying debris hits while you are driving, pull over and park. Stay in the car with your seat belt on. Put your head below the windows and cover it with your hands or a blanket.
  • Or, if you can safely get noticeably lower than the road, leave the car and lie in that ditch, covering your head with your arms.

After

  • Watch for downed power lines, broken glass, nails, and gas leaks.
  • Wear sturdy shoes, long pants, and gloves.
  • Text family instead of calling to keep lines open.

Thunderstorms and lightning

When thunder roars, go indoors. If you can hear thunder, you are close enough to be struck. Get into a building with plumbing and wiring, or a hard-topped car with windows up. Wait 30 minutes after the last thunder before going back out.
  • There is no safe place outside in a thunderstorm. Picnic shelters, tents, and trees don't protect you.
  • If you are caught outside, get off hills and ridges, away from water, tall lone trees, and metal fences. Keep moving toward safe shelter.
  • Indoors, stay off corded phones and away from plumbing, windows, and doors.
