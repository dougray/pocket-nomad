Do this now

  1. Stay indoors and off the roads if you can.
  2. If the power goes out, close off unused rooms and gather in one room. Hang blankets over windows and doors. Stuff towels under doors.
  3. Dress in layers. Wear a hat, even indoors. Sleep in a sleeping bag or under many blankets, together if you can.
  4. Eat and drink regularly. Food helps your body make heat.
  5. Never use a stove, oven, grill, or generator for heat indoors. Use a carbon monoxide alarm.
  6. Watch for hypothermia and frostbite, especially in children and older adults.

If stranded in a car

  1. Stay in the car. It is easier for rescuers to find you, and you are protected from the wind. Don't walk away unless help is very close and visible.
  2. Tie a bright cloth to the antenna or door. Turn on the dome light at night so rescuers can see you, if the battery allows.
  3. Run the engine and heater about 10 minutes each hour to stay warm.
  4. Clear snow from the exhaust pipe first. Crack a window on the side away from the wind while the engine runs. A blocked exhaust can fill the car with carbon monoxide.
  5. Move your arms and legs to keep blood flowing. Huddle together for warmth. Use floor mats, maps, or newspapers for extra insulation.
  6. Take turns sleeping so one person stays awake to watch.

Protect your home

  • Let faucets drip slowly to keep pipes from freezing. Open cabinet doors under sinks.
  • Know where your main water shutoff is in case a pipe bursts.
  • Keep extra blankets, food, water, batteries, and medicine.
  • Use fireplaces and wood stoves only if the chimney is clean. Keep a fire extinguisher handy.

Space heaters

  • Keep them at least 3 ft (1 m) from anything that can burn.
  • Plug electric heaters straight into the wall, not into an extension cord.
  • Turn them off when you leave the room or go to sleep.

Shoveling snow

Heavy snow shoveling can trigger heart attacks. Take breaks, push snow rather than lifting it, and stop if you feel chest pain or shortness of breath.

Alerts

  • Winter Storm Watch: a storm is possible. Get ready.
  • Winter Storm Warning: a dangerous storm is coming or happening. Stay off the roads.
  • Blizzard Warning: strong winds and blowing snow with very low visibility.
