Do this now

  1. Follow official orders. If told to evacuate, leave. If told to shelter in place, stay inside.
  2. Leave if your home is unsafe: fire nearby, rising water, gas leak, structural damage, or no way to stay warm or cool.
  3. Stay if going outside is more dangerous: tornado, chemical release, heavy smoke, or blocked roads.
  4. If you are lost outdoors, stay where you are, make shelter, and signal. Rescuers start where you were last seen.
  5. Whichever you choose, tell someone your plan and where you'll be.

If you evacuate

  1. Grab your go-bag, medicines, documents, phone and charger, and pets.
  2. Wear sturdy shoes and clothes for the weather.
  3. If time allows and officials say to: turn off gas, water, and electricity. Unplug appliances.
  4. Lock your home. Leave a note saying when you left and where you are going.
  5. Follow the routes officials give. Shortcuts may be blocked or flooded.
  6. Don't drive through flood water or around barricades.
  7. Text family and your out-of-area contact once you're safe.

If you shelter in place

Chemical release or radiation

  • Get inside, stay inside, stay tuned. Go into the nearest building.
  • Close and lock windows and doors. Turn off fans, heating, and air conditioning that pull in outside air.
  • Go to an interior room with few windows. For a chemical release, a room above ground level is better since many chemicals are heavier than air. For radiation, go to the basement or the middle of the building.
  • Seal gaps around windows, doors, and vents with plastic sheeting and tape if you're told to.
  • If you were outside during a radiation or chemical release, remove your outer clothing, bag it, and wash with soap and water.

Severe weather

Follow the Tornado, Hurricane, and Winter Storm guides.

Lost in the wilderness

  • Staying put makes you much easier to find. "Hug a tree" is the rule taught to children.
  • Move only if you must for safety, water, or shelter, or if you know exactly where help is. Leave a note or arrow showing which way you went.
Pick two meeting places with your family now: one near your home, and one outside your neighborhood. Choose one out-of-area contact everyone can text. See the Emergency Comms Plan.
