New: Ham radio. In the Radio tab, switch to Ham and pair a Bluetooth KISS TNC or a radio with one built in (BTech UV-Pro, Radioddity GA-5WB, Mobilinkd and more; classic-Bluetooth radios like the Kenwood TH-D75 too). Hear APRS stations, messages, bulletins and weather, and see stations on the map. Licensed hams can send Winlink email through an RMS gateway, chat over APRS and connect to packet BBSes. No callsign = listen only.
