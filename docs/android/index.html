Pocket Nomad for Android

Guides, tools, offline maps, mesh radio texting, coverage mapping and drive mode on Android. Try the beta now; Google Play is next.

Beta: install the APK

For testers who want it before it reaches Google Play. Android 8.0 or later.

Download the beta APK All releases and checksums

  1. On your Android phone, tap Download the beta APK above. Chrome may warn that this type of file can harm your device; tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file (from the notification, or Files › Downloads).
  3. If asked, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source for Chrome (or your browser), then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
  5. Allow location for the compass and map, and nearby devices when you pair a radio.
  6. Optional: turn Allow from this source back off for your browser under Settings › Apps › Special app access › Install unknown apps.

Updates: download and install the newest APK over the old one; your data stays. The Google Play version will be signed with the same key as this beta, so when it arrives, Play can update the beta in place and your data stays.

Found a problem? Report it on GitHub with your phone model and Android version.

Wear OS watch app (beta)

SOS light, compass and position, and the urgent first-aid steps on a Wear OS 3 or later watch. It works on its own, without the phone.

Download the Wear OS APK

Watches can't install an APK from a browser, so this takes a computer with Android's platform tools (free) on the same Wi-Fi as the watch:

  1. On the watch: Settings › System › About › tap Build number seven times to turn on Developer options.
  2. Settings › Developer options: turn on ADB debugging and Wireless debugging, then tap Pair new device.
  3. On the computer: adb pair IP:PORT with the address and code the watch shows.
  4. Then adb connect IP:PORT with the address on the Wireless debugging screen.
  5. Then adb install pocket-nomad-wear.apk, and turn Wireless debugging back off.

When the Google Play version is live, the watch app will install from Play on the watch instead.

What's in it

  • Survive: the same 69 guides and 8 checklists as the iPhone app, compass with MGRS, SOS and Morse light with the flashlight, water purifier, Medical ID, daylight.
  • Map: OpenStreetMap, download the area on screen for offline use, saved places, live mesh radios, known radios from meshmap.net and map.meshcore.io, coverage layer.
  • Mesh: Meshtastic and MeshCore over Bluetooth, channel chat, direct messages with delivery receipts, SOS, blocking.
  • Two radios: connect a Meshtastic and a MeshCore radio together; both map coverage and drive-mode pings at once, shown in their own colours.
  • Radio settings: change a radio's frequency, preset and transmit power from the app; "Match radios near you" picks the settings most radios around you use.
  • Drive mode: keeps mapping coverage with the screen off, with the ping test; every MeshCore packet heard becomes a map point, and MeshCore pings trace the nearest repeater. Shows a notification while it runs, picks up again if the app is closed, and stops itself after 4 hours.
  • Library: download Kiwix books (WikiMed, field medicine manuals, water treatment and more) and search all of them offline, or open a .zim file you already have.
  • Ask: plain-language questions answered by a free AI model that runs on the phone, only from your guides and books, with every source cited. Without the model, or on phones short of memory, it shows the best passages.
  • Wear OS: a separate watch app with the SOS light, compass and position, and the urgent first-aid steps. It works on its own, without the phone.
