Do this now

  1. Give small, frequent sips of fluid. Use oral rehydration solution (ORS) if you have it, or make it (recipe below).
  2. For vomiting, give a spoonful every 1 to 2 minutes. Slow and steady usually stays down.
  3. Keep breastfeeding babies. Breast milk helps.
  4. Rest in a cool place.
  5. Get medical help fast for any danger sign below, especially in babies, young children, and older adults.
Call 911 (or your local emergency number) as soon as you can. If you are alone with an adult, call first. If you can, put the phone on speaker so the dispatcher can coach you. This guide does not replace hands-on first aid training. Take a class from the Red Cross or American Heart Association when you can.

Get help now if

  • Very sleepy, confused, or hard to wake.
  • No urine for 8 hours or more (or no wet diaper in a baby for 6 hours or more). These numbers are a rough guide. Any big drop in urine is a warning.
  • Sunken eyes, no tears, very dry mouth.
  • Can't keep any fluids down, or blood in vomit or stool.
  • A baby has a sunken soft spot on the head.

Homemade oral rehydration solution (WHO recipe)

IngredientAmount
Clean, safe water1 liter (about 1 quart plus 2 tablespoons)
Sugar6 level teaspoons
Salt1/2 level teaspoon
  1. Use water that has been boiled and cooled, or treated (see Making Water Safe).
  2. Measure carefully with level spoons. Too much salt can be dangerous, especially for children.
  3. Stir until dissolved. It should taste no saltier than tears.
  4. Make a fresh batch every day. Throw out any left after 24 hours.
Store-bought ORS packets or drinks (like Pedialyte) are best if you have them. Sports drinks have too much sugar and too little salt for serious diarrhea. Water them down only if nothing else is available.

Signs of dehydration

  • Thirst, dry mouth, dark yellow urine, peeing less.
  • Headache, dizziness, tiredness.
  • In babies: fewer wet diapers, no tears, fussiness.

Diarrhea in a disaster

  • Keep drinking ORS after each loose stool.
  • Keep eating normal, simple food when able.
  • Wash hands well with soap. Diarrhea spreads easily. See the Sanitation guides.
