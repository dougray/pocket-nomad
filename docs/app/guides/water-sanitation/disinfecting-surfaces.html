Do this now

  1. Wear gloves. Open windows for fresh air.
  2. Clean dirt off first with soap and water. Disinfectants don't work well on dirty surfaces.
  3. Mix a bleach solution: 5 tablespoons (1/3 cup) of unscented bleach per gallon of water, or 4 teaspoons per quart (1 L).
  4. Wipe or spray it on. Leave it wet for at least 1 minute.
  5. Rinse food-contact surfaces with clean water and let air dry.
  6. Make a fresh batch every day. Bleach solution loses strength.
Never mix bleach with ammonia, vinegar, or any other cleaner. It makes toxic gas that can kill. Use cool or room-temperature water, not hot.

Strengths for different jobs

JobMix
Hard surfaces (counters, door handles, toilets)5 tablespoons (1/3 cup) bleach per gallon of water, or 4 tsp per quart
Flood cleanup: hard surfaces that touched flood water1 cup bleach per 5 gallons (19 L) of water
Sanitizing water containers1 tsp bleach per quart (1 L) of water

These mixes are for regular household bleach (5 to 9% sodium hypochlorite). Use only bleach labeled for disinfecting.

Tips

  • Clean the most-touched surfaces first: door handles, faucets, toilet handles, counters, and phones.
  • Bleach can damage fabric and metal. Rinse metal after.
  • Label your mixed bottles. Keep them away from children.
  • For a sick room, clean surfaces every day. Use separate cleaning cloths for the toilet area.
