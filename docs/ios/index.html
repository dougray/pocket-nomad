Set it up before you need it

Everything below needs a connection once; after that it all works with no signal.

  1. Install Pocket Nomad from the App Store and open it once.
  2. Medical ID: Survive › Medical ID. Add allergies, medicines and who to call.
  3. Maps: Library › Get Content › Maps. Download your city, region or state.
  4. Books: Library › Get Content. Start with WikiMed and the field medicine manuals.
  5. AI: on iPhone 15 Pro and newer, Apple's on-device model works with no download. Other iPhones: Library › Get Content › Qwen3 1.7B (or the 4B on an iPhone 15 or newer).
  6. Radio (optional): pair a Meshtastic or MeshCore radio under Mesh, then try Map › Layers › Download known radios.

What's in it

  • Survive: 69 guides checked against current first-aid guidance, 8 checklists, compass and MGRS, SOS and Morse light, water purifier, Medical ID, daylight.
  • Ask: plain-language questions answered only from your guides and books, with the source quoted.
  • Library: whole reference books offline with full-text search.
  • Map: OpenStreetMap offline, saved places, live mesh radios, known radios, coverage layer.
  • Mesh: Meshtastic and MeshCore texting, SOS with position, coverage scanner, drive mode with the ping test.
  • Apple Watch: SOS light, compass and position, urgent first-aid steps, quick mesh replies.
